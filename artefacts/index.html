Artefacts

Portfolio-created product artefacts

These templates demonstrate how I would run discovery, prioritisation, governance and measurement. They are not confidential employer documents.

  • Data Product Canvas

    Define a data product by consumer, decision, quality, access and success measures.

  • Product Vision

    Hold a one-page view of who the product is for and what changes if it works.

  • Opportunity Solution Tree

    Keep discovery attached to a desired outcome rather than a solution list.

  • User Journey

    Show where advisers, operations and product managers need information.

  • Service Blueprint

    Connect frontstage adviser/client moments to backstage data, controls and operations.

  • Problem Statement

    Force a problem to be specific enough to test.

  • Assumption Map

    Separate what is known from what must be tested.

  • Prioritisation Matrix

    Make value, risk, feasibility, data quality and adoption visible together.

  • Outcome-Based Roadmap

    Sequence learning and trust before scale.

  • Product Backlog Example

    Show how a data-product backlog is sliced by consumer outcome, not by pipeline task.

  • OKR Tree

    Connect north-star outcomes to product, quality and delivery measures without fake targets.

  • Metrics Framework

    Keep confirmed CV metrics separate from illustrative lab metrics.

  • Data Governance RACI

    Show product, data, engineering, risk, privacy and operations accountabilities.

  • Data Product Scorecard

    A weekly view of trust, adoption and action.

  • Risk and Dependency Register

    Make privacy, supplier, platform and adoption risks visible in the same register as delivery dependencies.

  • Release Readiness Checklist

    Prevent data products from launching without consumers, controls and support.

  • Product Review Template

    Run a review that inspects outcomes, not activity.
