About

Product leadership with a data-as-a-product mindset

I am not a former Data Engineer. I am a senior product and delivery leader who uses customer, operational, financial, risk, service, quality, adoption and performance data to shape products in financial services, wealth, platforms and regulated environments.

Professional summary

  • I connect customer and business problems to governed, measurable products.
  • I use customer, operational, service, financial, risk and delivery evidence to prioritise the opportunities that matter most.
  • I work closely with Product, Engineering, Data, Operations, Risk and Compliance to move from discovery through delivery, adoption and continuous improvement.

Product leadership philosophy

  • Product work starts with a clearly framed problem, not a requested feature.
  • Data becomes valuable when a named consumer can use it to make a better decision.
  • Prioritisation should make value, risk, feasibility and capacity visible at the same time.
  • Release is not the outcome. Adoption, control readiness and measured benefit are.
  • Governance should create pace and trust, not theatre.

Data-product mindset

  • Treat priority datasets as products with consumers, owners, quality expectations and success measures.
  • Separate confirmed employment evidence from transferable capability and portfolio demonstrations.
  • Use Power BI and product-performance analysis where confirmed, and partner with engineering and data specialists for platform depth.
  • Design privacy, access, lineage and control evidence into the product conversation rather than adding them at the end.

Working style

  • Evidence-led and commercially practical.
  • Comfortable in matrix organisations and regulated environments.
  • Coach product and delivery professionals on outcome-based roadmaps, backlog quality and product metrics.
  • Balance day-to-day delivery with multi-year portfolio thinking.

Leadership principles

  • Clarify the decision that the product needs to support.
  • Make trade-offs explicit across customer value, commercial outcome, risk and capacity.
  • Keep ownership visible from discovery through operational acceptance.
  • Measure what changes for the user, the operator and the business.

Industry experience
